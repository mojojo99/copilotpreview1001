Part 3 · Max out free Chat

Prompting is just
giving a better brief.

What needs doing?

Summarise, draft, compare, plan.

What does good look like?

Audience, facts, tone, length, format.

What stays uncertain?

Unconfirmed items → "to confirm".

If a colleague would need to ask, Copilot needs the context too.
One reusable pattern

Role · Task · Context · Format

Before

"Write an announcement for our training."

Copilot will guess the audience, facts and length.

Role: Internal comms assistant. Task: Draft a training announcement. Context: For managers. Registration required. Date not confirmed. Format: 3 bullets, under 80 words. Mark missing details "to confirm".
Two power moves

Let it ask. Show an example.

Ask first

"Before drafting, ask me up to 3 questions that would most change the result."

For anything with an audience, a deadline or a decision.

Show an example

"Match the tone of this: 'Please confirm the list by Thursday so we can finalise the room. Thank you.'"

One example beats five adjectives.

Ask for formula, check, and honesty.

Demo 2 · Catch the convincing mistake

What's the overall collection rate?

A80.0%: the average of 90%, 60%, 80%, 90%
B83.8%: RM335k collected ÷ RM400k invoiced
✓ B is right. Every ringgit counts once; a simple average gives small Penang the same weight as KL. =SUM(C2:C5)/SUM(B2:B5) → 83.75%, about 1.3 points below target.
